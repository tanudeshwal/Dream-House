Why Dream House?

This project is designed to make basic house planning easier for middle-income and low-income families. It helps users understand how their available space and budget can be used before they move to detailed professional planning.

Important: Cost, quantities and layouts shown by this student project are preliminary estimates. Final structural design, construction quantities and local approvals should be handled by qualified professionals.
